ProgChamp is an online platform for users to publish and enjoy a collection of various games, sortable by genre and carefully curated. Users can rate the games, comment on them, and to show some extra love, they can even boost the games' popularity by superliking up to 3 of them! To spice things up, ProgChamp will also be hosting seasonal challenges that aspiring game developers can join and compete in. It can potentially be expanded beyond games to other user-generated content as well :D
